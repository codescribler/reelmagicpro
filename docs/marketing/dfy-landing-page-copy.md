The parent has hours of Veo footage and no finished reels. They have either tried DaVinci/CapCut and given up, or they have not started because they know they will give up. The footage that should land in front of scouts is sitting in a Downloads folder.

Order a reel (£79 / £129 / £249). Single conversion path. No subscription, no season pass, no commitment beyond the reel being ordered.

## 7. OBJECTION HANDLING (FAQ)

**Q: My match isn't on Veo, or my Veo is behind a club login. Can you still make a reel?**
Yes. Footage can come from anywhere — Veo, an iPhone on the sideline, a friend's camcorder, a DVD rip. The only constraint is that we need to be able to download it. If your Veo share link is public (most are), paste it into the order email. If it sits behind a club login, or your footage is anywhere else, drop the mp4 into your own Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer and send the share link instead. We do not accept direct file uploads — every order is link-based.

**Q: What if you do not pick the moments I would have picked?**
You pick the moments. The intake form asks for timestamps — "3:42 — through ball, 27:10 — tackle that won the corner." We work from your list, not our judgement. If the first cut misses what you wanted, the included round of revisions sorts it.

**Q: What about footage I filmed on my phone, not Veo?**
Phone footage is fine — most parents have a mix. Use a Compilation Reel if you want to combine phone clips with the Veo match. Single Reel and Match Showcase are designed around one source per order to keep the price honest.

**Q: What music can I use — and what about copyright?**
You supply the audio file as a link (your own Google Drive, Dropbox, or similar). You are responsible for having the rights to use the track. We do not check or vet copyright on supplied music, and we are not responsible for any rights issues that follow from using it on a reel that gets shared. If you have not got a track in mind, the routes most parents use are royalty-free libraries (the YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music) or AI music tools that grant a personal-use licence. The £79 Single Reel is no-music by design — short scout clips often land harder without one.

**Q: What happens to my footage after the reel is delivered?**
The source video is deleted from the founder's machine within 14 days of delivery. We keep the finished mp4 for 90 days in case you want a re-download, then that goes too. We never use your child's footage in marketing without explicit written permission, and we will never ask.

**Q: What if I am not happy with the first cut?**
One round of revisions is included with every order — change the moments, swap the music, retime the slow-mo. We would rather you get the reel you wanted than save the half-hour. If a second round is needed, it is £25; that has only happened once.

**Q: Why pay for this when I could buy the editor and do it myself?**
You should, if you have an evening free and you want to learn the app. The app exists, it works, and most Done For You customers end up using it themselves eventually. This service exists for the matches you do not have the evening for, the reels you needed yesterday, and the parents who have already tried and would rather not try again.

**Q: I have a scout who needs the reel by Friday. Can you actually do it in 24 hours?**
Yes. A standard Single Reel takes about 20 minutes of work end-to-end; the 24-hour window is comfortable. If your match is on Sunday and your scout meeting is Tuesday, order Sunday evening, the reel is back Monday.

**Overall voice:** Plain English, sideline-feel. The founder is a parent talking to other parents who are in the same gap — not an agency selling up.

**Avoid:**

- Emoji in body copy (the No-Fakes Pledge anti-feature carries over)

- Pre-ticked checkboxes, urgency timers, "only X spots left"

- The buyer's verbatim pain quotes where they fit ("It's been so hard to do that it just doesn't get done")

- Specific football-pathway nouns (JPL, APDC, U13, U14, Veo, Cassiobury Rangers, Watford)
