## What's new

- **Record dynamic zooms:** follow the action with your pointer and use the mouse wheel to zoom into details or pull back for context. Camera movement is gently smoothed and reproduced in previews and exports. Left-click the video to start, click again to save and replay, or press Escape to cancel.
- **Save named sequences inside your project:** create, copy, rename and switch between arrangements, each with its own clip order, music and brightness. Save the project to reopen and edit all your sequences later.
- **Drag to reorder sequences:** drop clips between other clips or at either end, with a visible insertion line and automatic scrolling for long sequences.
- **Adjust player-tag text size:** reduce names to 10–100% of their original size, with matching background padding, in previews and exports.
- New player tracks default to **Strong** smoothing.
- The Start/End clip control now sits to the left of Play.

## Improvements

- Dynamic zoom preserves variable-frame-rate video duration and slow-motion timing.
- Cancelling a replacement zoom take preserves the previous recording.
- Existing projects retain their clips, sequence and settings; the original arrangement is available as Sequence 1 when naming sequences.
- Reordering retains the selected sequence occurrence, including repeated clips.
